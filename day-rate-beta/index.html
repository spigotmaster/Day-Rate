How scoring works

Mood, Productivity and Connection are each scored 0 to 5 against your own history. Your highest rating ever in a category earns 5, your lowest earns 0. The three add up to a score out of 15. Past scores shift as your range grows. With one day or identical ratings, each category gets a neutral 2.5.

How Insights work

Insights use only the ratings stored on this device and are calculated here, with no account or server. They describe patterns in your own history and never prove what causes what. Summaries need 7 rated days, pattern insights need 14 days with all three ratings, and findings built on fewer than 30 days are marked preliminary. Notes are only scanned for a few everyday keywords such as walk, gym or friends.

Research behind Insights

Several insights are built on published studies. They describe groups of people, so Day Rate only checks whether the same pattern shows in your own ratings.

Progress and mood: Amabile and Kramer (2011). Happiness and output: Oswald, Proto and Sgroi (2015). Daily social contact: Sandstrom and Dunn (2014). Weekends: Helliwell and Wang (2014). Writing down good things: Seligman and colleagues (2005). A later replication found lasting happiness gains too, but a placebo exercise did as well.
